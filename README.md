# Sleepwalk

A short first-person walk through a dream recorded on a worn VHS tape. The rooms are bigger on the inside: a booth
whose doorway opens onto a 140-metre corridor, a shed with a swimming pool in it, a door standing alone in a field
that goes somewhere different from each side, a dollhouse you can walk into, a corridor with no end, and a model
town with a snow globe you can walk into as well. No loading screens: you just walk through the doorways. And the
dream doesn't hold still: every time you cross into another place, the one you left changes a little while
nobody's looking.

**Play it at https://demidummy.github.io/sleepwalk/** (desktop browser, keyboard and mouse; headphones recommended).

WASD to walk, mouse to look, Shift to run, Space to jump, E to interact, M to mute, Esc to pause.

This repo only holds the built game. It's published from a private source repo by `tools/release-pages.sh`.
